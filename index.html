II.
Brand Agents

Digital employees for property management. We design, build, and deploy the agentic workforce that runs your portfolio.

The Offer

Hire a team
that doesn't sleep.

Property management runs on repetitive cognitive labor — answering inquiries, scheduling tours, screening applicants, drafting renewals, fielding maintenance triage. Most of it can be done by agents that are always on, always consistent, and answer in seconds.

Apateu builds these agents on top of your existing PMS. They speak to prospects, talk to residents, and write to your systems through tool calls. They aren't chatbots — they're scoped digital employees with memory, retrieval, and explicit permissions.

We work hands-on with a small number of design partners per quarter. You bring the portfolio and the pain. We bring the agents.
